# Copy check

From the Claude Design Blueprint, hack 12 (sources: Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing", github.com/blader/humanizer, github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop).

Score the copy out of 10, fix, score again. Cut:
- Hype words: seamless, robust, leverage, unlock, elevate, game-changer, cutting-edge, revolutionize, empower.
- Filler openers ("In today's fast-paced world"), and closers that restate the point.
- Rule-of-three lists that pad, em dashes as decoration, vague "solutions".
Keep: short sentences, plain words, the reader's own words, one idea per line, a concrete next step.
