Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE-DATE-TBD]

1. Who we are

LAVAALL ("LAVAALL," "we," "us") supplies enterprise IT hardware and solutions to businesses across Sierra Leone, Guinea, Liberia, and Guinea-Bissau. This policy explains what information lavaall.com collects and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

Our Contact Us form asks for personal information. A text assistant, when available, may also collect the inquiry information described below. Depending on what you fill in, that can include your name, email address, phone number, company name, country, the type of inquiry, your message, how you'd prefer to be contacted, and — if you request a callback — your preferred date and time. Every field is either required so we can respond to you, or clearly marked optional.

We don't ask visitors to create an account, and we don't run advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or third-party tracking pixels on the site. The form stores a timestamp in your browser, used only to stop the same device from submitting the form repeatedly in a short window — basic spam protection. It isn't tied to your identity and isn't sent anywhere.

3. How we use your information

We use what you submit for one purpose: to respond to you. Based on the inquiry type you select, your message is routed to the right team inbox — support, sales, orders, partnerships, or general information — so the right person gets back to you. We may also use your contact details to follow up on a quote, order, or support request you've already started with us.

4. How your information is handled and stored

When you submit the form, it's delivered by email into our company's Google Workspace mailboxes. Your message is stored the same way any of our business email is stored, under Google's own security and infrastructure — which may process data on servers outside Sierra Leone, Guinea, Liberia, or Guinea-Bissau as part of how Google Workspace normally operates. Chat may also use temporary application records as described below.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information. It's seen only by LAVAALL staff who need it to answer your inquiry, by Google as the infrastructure that delivers our email, and, when text chat is available, by DeepSeek, Vercel (hosting and, if configured, AI Gateway — TO-CONFIRM-BY-OSMAN), and Google Workspace, as described below. We do not share inquiry information with third-party marketers or data brokers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and order-related correspondence for as long as it's useful to our relationship with you — generally as a record of quotes, orders, and support history. If you'd like your information deleted, contact us and we'll do so unless we're required to keep it (for example, for an active order or a legal obligation).

7. Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@lavaall.com (open in mail app) or info@lavaall.com (open in mail app). If you reach us through WhatsApp, Telegram, or Messenger, keep in mind those platforms have their own privacy terms on top of this one.

Text assistant

When it is available, LAVAALL Assistant is an AI text assistant. It can make mistakes. You may use the Contact Us form, email, or human WhatsApp instead. Do not share passwords, payment card details, identification documents, or other sensitive information in chat.

If you use the assistant, we collect your chat messages (the text you type and the assistant's replies) and, if you ask to send a request to the team, the details you provide for that request: your name; a WhatsApp number or an email address (you choose one); the topic of the request; and a short summary of what you need. Optional details you may also give, when you use the longer inquiry form, are: inquiry type, country, company, subject, message, preferred contact method (email or phone), product or service, model, quantity, budget, delivery location, timeline, order number, issue description, installation type, and project description. For a request sent from the in-chat handoff, your name and either a WhatsApp number or an email address are required, along with the topic. Nothing is emailed to the team until you review the details, agree to the use of your request for follow-up, and confirm. Preparing a draft, or chatting without confirming a request, does not send an inquiry.

Chat runs on Vercel, which hosts lavaall.com and the chat service. Replies are generated by DeepSeek (model deepseek-flash). If that call cannot be completed, the same messages may be sent to Vercel AI Gateway as a fallback. TO-CONFIRM-BY-OSMAN: whether a Gateway key is configured on the environment that would serve chat and what that fallback retains. When production delivery is later enabled, a confirmed in-chat handoff will send one email to support@lavaall.com through Google Workspace and create one private row in LAVAALL Customer Requests / All Requests, with Source Page set to chatbot. Those records follow the correspondence retention in section 6. Preview and unset transport use a temporary test-sink record instead. The Apps Script change in this stack has not been deployed; this notice does not mean production delivery is live today.

Your chat text is sent to DeepSeek so it can generate a reply. DeepSeek's public Privacy Policy (last update February 10, 2026, https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html, fetched November 15, 2026) states: “To provide you with our services, we directly collect, process and store your Personal Data in People's Republic of China.” That is a material fact. The same policy does not publish a fixed retention period: it keeps personal data for as long as necessary to provide the services and for other listed purposes, and it lists improving and training its models as a purpose, with a right to opt out. It also says that policy does not itself cover end users of applications that developers build on DeepSeek's open platform, and that the developer must disclose how personal data is handled — which this page does. No English Open Platform privacy policy was available at the expected URL on this date (that URL returned 404).

To keep your chat session, the site sets a first-party cookie named lavaall_chat. It holds a secret session proof, not your name or message text. It is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, Secure, and scoped to the path /api/chat-inquiry. It lasts 30 minutes (Max-Age=1800). The matching server session, including recent chat text and any draft request, is kept for 30 minutes. A short-lived review token, used only to confirm a request, expires after 2 minutes or when the session ends, whichever is sooner. Rate-limit counters use a salted hash of your IP address, not the raw address, and expire at the end of their hourly or daily window. A confirmed in-chat handoff record, when written to the temporary application store, is kept for 24 hours. A monthly spend counter used to cap chat costs contains no message text or contact details and is kept until the start of the calendar month after next (UTC). Application expiry does not delete a DeepSeek or Gateway transcript, or an email or private request row once one has been sent or created.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information. To ask what chat information we hold, to correct it, or to delete it, email support@lavaall.com. We will explain any records that must be kept (for example an email already delivered, or a legal obligation) and will request deletion from a provider where that option is available.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses, and the site isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and change the effective date above. We won't make a material change without giving you a reasonable way to see it first.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy: info@lavaall.com (open in mail app) or support@lavaall.com (open in mail app).
