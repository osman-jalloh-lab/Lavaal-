What to include in your quote request

A clear brief helps define the options you want compared. Send the following details, even if some specifications are still open:

  • Preferred brand and model, or the specification you need.
  • Quantity for each item or configuration.
  • New or refurbished preference.
  • Delivery location in Sierra Leone.
  • Your project deadline.
  • Equipment categories and any requirements shared across the order.

If you have an equipment list already, use it as the basis of your message. Identify any required details so alternatives can be discussed before you approve.

How quoting works

  1. Send your request. Share your equipment brief and contact details.
  2. Review the written options. We source options and confirm price, availability and payment options in a written quote.
  3. You approve. Review the proposed equipment and terms before approving the quote.

Questions before you request a quote

Do I need an exact model to request a quote?

No. You can describe your requirements and preferred brand so we can source options for written review.

Can I combine categories in one request?

Yes. List quantities and specifications by category, and explain any requirements shared across the project.

How are purchase details confirmed?

Review the written quote for the proposed equipment and payment options. You approve after considering those details.
