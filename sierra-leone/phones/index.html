Phones sourced on request

Brands and families we can source or quote on request include Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Tecno, Infinix, Motorola, Xiaomi and OnePlus. A listing is a starting point for a conversation about sourcing, rather than a commitment to a particular device. Ask us to confirm the exact model and configuration in writing.

Include storage, colour and connectivity preferences when they matter to your purchase. If your team needs identical devices, say so and provide the quantity. If a suitable alternative would work, explain which specifications are essential and which preferences can change. This helps keep the sourcing request focused on the devices you actually need.

State whether you prefer new or refurbished phones. Do not infer condition from the model name or category image: the model and condition must be confirmed for your request. For an order that also needs computers or network equipment, use the IT procurement guide to prepare an itemised list across categories.

What to include in your quote request

A clear brief helps define the options you want compared. Send the following details, even if some specifications are still open:

  • Preferred brand and model, or the specification you need.
  • Quantity for each item or configuration.
  • New or refurbished preference.
  • Delivery location in Sierra Leone.
  • Your project deadline.
  • Preferred phone model, storage, colour, connectivity and whether matching devices are required.

If you have an equipment list already, use it as the basis of your message. Identify any required details so alternatives can be discussed before you approve.

How quoting works

  1. Send your request. Share your equipment brief and contact details.
  2. Review the written options. We source options and confirm price, availability and payment options in a written quote.
  3. You approve. Review the proposed equipment and terms before approving the quote.

Questions before you request a quote

Does a phone listing mean the device is ready to buy?

No. Listings are sourcing requests and the phone catalogue is still being verified. Models and conditions are confirmed per quote.

Can I request new or refurbished phones?

Yes. State your preference; the exact condition and configuration need to be confirmed in the written quote.

Can I request phones for a business team?

Yes. Give the quantity and explain whether devices must match or whether alternatives can be considered.
