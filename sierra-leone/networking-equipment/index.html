Routers, switches, Wi-Fi and cabling

Brands and equipment we can source or quote include Cisco routers and switches, MikroTik routers, Ubiquiti access points and Fortinet firewalls. Specify a preferred model when replacing existing equipment, or describe the required configuration when you need options. A brand name alone does not define the right device for your network.

For switches, list the number of ports and any power-over-Ethernet requirements. For routers and firewalls, describe the connections and functions you need. For Wi-Fi access points, explain the site layout, the areas to cover and the expected number of users or devices. Include existing equipment details when compatibility matters.

Structured cabling and fiber can be included in the same sourcing request. Identify cable types, approximate lengths, connectors and any termination requirements you already know. Browse networking equipment and fiber and cabling for category context. If installation is part of your project, describe it separately so the requested scope is clear.

What to include in your quote request

A clear brief helps define the options you want compared. Send the following details, even if some specifications are still open:

  • Preferred brand and model, or the specification you need.
  • Quantity for each item or configuration.
  • New or refurbished preference.
  • Delivery location in Sierra Leone.
  • Your project deadline.
  • Port counts, connection types, Wi-Fi requirements, cabling lengths and existing equipment.

If you have an equipment list already, use it as the basis of your message. Identify any required details so alternatives can be discussed before you approve.

How quoting works

  1. Send your request. Share your equipment brief and contact details.
  2. Review the written options. We source options and confirm price, availability and payment options in a written quote.
  3. You approve. Review the proposed equipment and terms before approving the quote.

Questions before you request a quote

Can I request equipment from different brands?

Yes. List each item separately and explain any compatibility requirements between devices.

Can cabling be included with network hardware?

Yes. Include structured cabling or fiber requirements, quantities and connectors in your request.

What if I do not have a model number?

Describe the function, connections and required capacity. We can source options for you to review before approval.
