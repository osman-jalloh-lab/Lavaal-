Business and everyday laptop sourcing

Brands and families we can source or quote include Dell Latitude and XPS, HP EliteBook, ProBook and ZBook, Lenovo ThinkPad, IdeaPad and Yoga, Apple MacBook Air and MacBook Pro, Acer Aspire, ASUS VivoBook and Microsoft Surface. These are sourcing options; the exact model and configuration must be confirmed in your quote.

For bulk laptops for offices, schools or NGOs, explain whether everyone needs the same configuration or different specifications by role. Business lines and everyday consumer lines can be considered against your requirements. Include the software you expect to use, preferred screen size, memory and storage rather than relying only on a family name.

State whether you want new equipment, refurbished equipment or options for both. Condition is confirmed per quote. If a particular configuration is essential, mark it as required; if alternatives are acceptable, explain which details can change.

Brand names are trademarks of their owners and do not imply an authorized partnership.

What to include in your quote request

A clear brief helps define the options you want compared. Send the following details, even if some specifications are still open:

  • Preferred brand and model, or the specification you need.
  • Quantity for each item or configuration.
  • New or refurbished preference.
  • Delivery location in Sierra Leone.
  • Your project deadline.
  • Preferred screen size, processor, RAM, storage and software requirements.

If you have an equipment list already, use it as the basis of your message. Identify any required details so alternatives can be discussed before you approve.

How quoting works

  1. Send your request. Share your equipment brief and contact details.
  2. Review the written options. We source options and confirm price, availability and payment options in a written quote.
  3. You approve. Review the proposed equipment and terms before approving the quote.

Questions before you request a quote

Can I request laptops for a whole team?

Yes. Send quantities by role or specification, including any requirement for matching models. We will source options against that request.

Can I compare business and consumer lines?

Yes. Explain your use case and preferred families so the written quote can identify the options being considered.

Are refurbished laptops an option?

You can request new or refurbished options. The model, configuration and condition are confirmed per quote.
