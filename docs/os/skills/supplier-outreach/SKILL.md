# Supplier Outreach

Draft supplier first-contact messages. Tone: casual, phone-first — looking to source products, ship to West Africa, get on a call — not formal RFQ language.

## Sender check (Osman OK 20 Nov 8:57 AM CT)
Before a draft is marked ready, name the From address and keep LAVAALL and SaloneHire mail separate:
- **LAVAALL** (suppliers, restaurants/hotels, artists, partners, and other LAVAALL outreach): Osman sends from support@lavaall.com. Never the Austin CC Gmail.
- **SaloneHire** (jobs platform, employers, applicants, SaloneHire partners): Osman sends from @salonehire.com. Never support@lavaall.com, and never the Austin CC Gmail.
- Replies follow the same split. Agents never send.

## Osman voice (required on every outreach draft, added 16 Oct 2026)
Applies to all LAVAALL outreach drafts (suppliers, restaurants/hotels, artists, partners).
- Header line: `DRAFT – Osman sends`. Saved as a draft card for Osman to send himself from the address in the sender check above. **Agents never send.** Never use the Austin CC Gmail for LAVAALL or SaloneHire.
- Warm tone. Open with a genuine compliment about their business.
- Right after the compliment, say what the owner concretely gets (e.g. "a free preview of a booking page your guests can use on their phone"). Never open with, or include, a list of our services.
- Never list a site's faults or problems. Frame everything as what they gain.
- Intro: "I'm Osman Jalloh, I run LAVAALL." Never say he's from Africa (or Sierra Leone).
- No invented stats or prices. No Starlink or Guinea in public copy.
- Always end with: "Would you like a free preview?"
- Contact line: `[REDACTED] · support@lavaall.com` for LAVAALL drafts. SaloneHire drafts use @salonehire.com and never support@.
- Before Jev scoring, list risks/costs in one line (most 15 Oct FLAGs were weakest there).

## Address checks (required before any draft is marked ready, added 30 Oct 2026)
1. MX lookup on the email domain (e.g. `dig +short MX domain`). No MX record = fail.
2. The exact address must appear on the venue's own website/page (not a directory or third-party listing). Record the URL where it was seen.
3. Any address that fails either check: mark **HOLD – WhatsApp fallback**. WhatsApp is **paused for now**, so held contacts wait; don't draft a WhatsApp send until Osman lifts the pause.
4. Note the check result (MX pass/fail, source URL) at the top of each draft.

## SaloneHire / SL package drafts
When a draft mentions SaloneHire or SL packages: the packages are $30–35, shown in US dollars and Leones, using a cited official Bank of Sierra Leone rate with the date of the rate (never invent the Leone figure). Nothing is published without Osman's OK.

## Hard stops
- **Nothing gets sent.** Drafts only.
- Draft = L1. **Send** (email/WhatsApp/Messenger/call script delivery) = **L3** — stop and ask founder.
- Do not claim brand authorizations, territories, or pricing you cannot cite.
- Prefer suppliers already scored via supplier-evaluation when available.

## Draft ingredients
- Who we are (quote-first IT/hardware sourcing for West Africa; Sierra Leone first)
- What we want (product class / named models if known)
- Ship-to region interest (SL, then GN/GW/LR as relevant)
- Ask for a short call or WhatsApp
- No payment negotiation in first touch (payments after quote: bank, Orange Money, AfriMoney, MoneyGram — agree later)

## Steps
1. Confirm target supplier + channel + goal in one line.
2. Pull evaluation notes / primary-source territory facts if present.
3. Write short casual draft (EN; FR only if supplier market requires).
4. Run plain-rephrase if the draft sounds formal or long.
5. **Stop.** Present draft + permission-gate L3 send ask. Do not send.

## Completion criteria
- [ ] Draft ready and casual/phone-first
- [ ] No invented authorizations or prices
- [ ] Sender check met (LAVAALL from support@; SaloneHire from @salonehire.com, never support@)
- [ ] Osman voice block met (compliment, concrete owner benefit first, no service list, no faults, no Africa mention, ends with "Would you like a free preview?", contact line, DRAFT – Osman sends)
- [ ] Address checks done (MX + on venue's own page); failures on HOLD – WhatsApp fallback (paused)
- [ ] Explicit L3 stop before send
