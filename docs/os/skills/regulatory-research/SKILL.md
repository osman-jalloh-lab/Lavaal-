# Regulatory Research (LAVAALL)

Evaluate a **specific business activity** in a **specific jurisdiction** using official and primary sources. Never treat AI-generated regulatory advice as legal authority.

Builds on [research-primary](sand-workflow:research-primary) with a regulatory lens.

## Scope examples
- Import / customs / duties for IT hardware into Sierra Leone, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, or Liberia
- Reseller / distributor eligibility (e.g. Starlink — currently **not authorized**)
- Payment method rules (bank, Orange Money, AfriMoney, MoneyGram) at a high level
- Data/privacy notices for the public site (flag professional review when needed)

## Steps
1. Name: **activity** + **jurisdiction** + **business question** in one sentence.
2. Search official gazettes, customs, telecom, and government portals first; then manufacturers/distributors; then reputable secondary — labeled as secondary.
3. Record each material claim with: claim, source, URL, effective date, date checked, confidence, implication.
4. Separate: **what the sources say** vs **what LAVAALL should do** vs **what needs a lawyer/licensed advisor**.
5. Output a short founder brief: findings, risks, open questions, recommended next step (often grill-founder or no-go until counsel).
6. Save under OS/discovery research notes; promote durable facts via domain-glossary / context only when CONFIRMED.

## Hard rules
- No “you are legally cleared to …” from this skill alone
- No filings, applications, or commitments (L3–L4)
- Starlink and similar: research OK; acting as authorized reseller requires explicit founder + any required licenses

## Permission
- L1 research and briefing
- L3–L4 for filings, contracts, or public regulatory claims

## Completion criteria
- [ ] Activity + jurisdiction named
- [ ] Primary/official sources prioritized
- [ ] Claim records complete for material points
- [ ] Professional-review flag set when appropriate
- [ ] No unauthorized legal conclusions or fake certainty
