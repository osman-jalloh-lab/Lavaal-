**Subject: Request for written guidance on licensing for Starlink equipment sales, installation and related services**

Dear Director-General,

I am writing on behalf of LAVAALL, a technology company serving customers in Sierra Leone. We plan to help homes and businesses get connected with Starlink satellite internet. We want to make sure we do this fully within Sierra Leone's regulations before we offer any service.

We understand from NatCA's public communication in September 2024 that resellers, retailers and installers of Starlink services require a licence from the Authority. We would be grateful for your written guidance on the following activities, each considered on its own:

1. **Supply of equipment:** selling Starlink kits (terminal, router, cable, power supply) that LAVAALL buys from Starlink or its authorized channels, where the customer holds their own Starlink service account directly with Starlink.
2. **Installation only:** mounting, aligning and configuring a Starlink kit that the customer already owns and has bought directly from Starlink.
3. **Related services only:** supplying and installing power protection (UPS/surge protection), Wi-Fi/mesh networking, cabling and mounts around a customer's own Starlink kit, without selling the kit or the Starlink service.
4. **Advertising:** describing the services above on our website and social media, with a clear statement that LAVAALL is independent and not affiliated with Starlink or SpaceX.

For each activity, could you please confirm:
- whether a licence, authorization or registration from NatCA is required;
- if so, which licence category applies, the application process, fees and the usual processing time;
- whether imported terminals and equipment need type approval, and who is responsible for it (LAVAALL or the customer);
- any conditions we must follow, for example on branding, record-keeping or customer information.

We will not offer Starlink sales or installation services in Sierra Leone until we have your answer, and we will apply for any licence the Authority requires. We would be glad to meet your licensing team, and can provide our company documents on request.

Thank you for your time and guidance. We look forward to your written reply.
