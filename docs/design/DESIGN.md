## Type
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque (600 to 800), tight tracking (-0.02em) for big headings.
- Text: Inter 400 to 600. Labels: a monospace, uppercase, 0.08 to 0.14em tracking, small.
- Two families on any page, never more.

## Shape and space
Cards 14px radius, controls 8px, pills 999px. Spacing scale 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. One filled accent button per screen.

## Motion
150 to 300ms, ease-out `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`. Hover lift 4px, press 0.97, 40ms stagger. Reduced motion turns it off.

## Voice
Plain, short, warm, direct. Krio-friendly English for Sierra Leone audiences. No hype words. No invented numbers, prices, testimonials or law.

## Businesses
LAVAALL (electronics and IT sourcing, Sierra Leone), SaloneWorks and SaloneHire (recruitment; SaloneHire launches 1 Dec 2026). The private business is never named in public copy.
